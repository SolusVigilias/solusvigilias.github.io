# Study & Research

A reusable workspace for studying representations, transformations, and evolving geometry. Begin with general objects and assumptions, then work through an example and test your understanding.

<p><a class="lab-link" href="{{ '/study/workspace/' | relative_url }}">Open the interactive workspace &rarr;</a></p>

## Seven guided modules

Every module includes precise notation, a compact intuition, a worked example, and a checkable exercise with space for your reasoning and feedback.

1. **[Compression sufficiency](/study/workspace/?module=0)** — Preserve the conditional law of a declared target.
2. **[Sufficient-state representations](/study/workspace/?module=1)** — Check prediction and observation-conditioned recursive updating separately.
3. **[Operator sequences](/study/workspace/?module=2)** — Make composition order and domains explicit.
4. **[Equivalence classes](/study/workspace/?module=3)** — Determine which maps and updates descend to a quotient.
5. **[Phase alignment](/study/workspace/?module=4)** — Study a declared complex-phase comparison model, including nonunique alignment.
6. **[Parallel transport](/study/workspace/?module=5)** — Compare tangent vectors using a specified connection and path.
7. **[State–metric co-updates](/study/workspace/?module=6)** — Distinguish changes to a state, its metric, and its coordinates.

## A research log that preserves uncertainty

Keep **documented commitments**, **open questions**, **tentative hypotheses**, and **next steps** separate. Commitments require a source or an explicit decision. Teaching examples and working models are not automatically treated as established MirrorVerse claims.

Record a session in your own words to revisit where you stopped. Viewing a module or passing an exercise never marks it understood; progress changes only when you report it.

## Your notebook

This section is publicly accessible. Your answers, notes, research entries, and session records are stored locally in your browser, not uploaded to GitHub or shared with other visitors. They do not automatically sync across devices.
